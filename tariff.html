Terms & Conditions

Charges & Conditions

All additional charges explained clearly — no fine prin surprises.

Driver Bata — One Way

₹500 per trip for Sedan & SUV. ₹600 per trip for Innova & Crysta. Waiting charges are ₹150 per hour.

Driver Bata — Round Trip

Per day: ₹500 Sedan & SUV, ₹600 Innova & Crysta, ₹800 Tempo Traveller, ₹1,000 Urbania. 1 day = 1 calendar day (00:00 to 18:00 hrs).

Minimum Running KM

One way trips: 130 km coverage / minimum billing. Round trips: 250 km coverage per day. Actual km apply if more.

Hill Station Charges

₹300 extra for Sedan. ₹500 extra for SUV & larger vehicles. Applicable for hill station destinations due to terrain and wear.

Night Charges

₹200 extra. Applicable only for one way trips that start between 11 PM and 5 AM. Round trips are not subject to night charges.

Pet Washing Charges

₹400 for Sedan and ₹500 for larger vehicles, applicable when travelling with pets. This covers post-trip vehicle cleaning.

Luggage Charges

₹300 for carrying extra or oversized luggage beyond the standard allowance. Check our Luggage Policy for full details.

Extra Pickup / Drop Points

Only 2 points (pickup & drop) are included. More than 2 stops will incur additional charges as applicable.

Extra Passenger Charges

₹300 per extra person beyond the standard vehicle capacity. Please inform us at the time of booking.

* All rates are GST inclusive. Toll fees and Inter-State Permit charges (if any) are extra.

FAQ

Pricing Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about our tariff and billing.

No, toll and parking charges are collected at actual cost. We clearly mention this upfront before you book. Our drivers use FASTag where available to avoid delays, and you'll receive a receipt for all charges paid during the trip.

Driver allowance is a fixed daily charge that covers the driver's food and stay during outstation trips. It depends on the vehicle: ₹500 for Sedan & SUV, ₹600 for Innova & Crysta, ₹800 for Tempo Traveller and ₹1,000 for Urbania (Tempo & Urbania are round trip only). This is clearly shown in your fare breakdown before booking and does not change after confirmation.

Yes! We understand travel plans change. You can modify your return date up to 12 hours before the originally scheduled return time. Simply call or WhatsApp our support team and we'll adjust the booking. Any fare difference due to additional days will be settled directly.

A nominal night surcharge of ₹200 applies for trips that begin or have a pickup between 11 PM and 5 AM. This covers the driver's safety allowance during late hours.

For one-way trips: Total Fare = (Distance × Per KM Rate) + Driver Bata + Toll/Parking (actual). A minimum of 130 KM is billed. There's no return fare charged. For example, a 200 km one-way Sedan trip: 200 × ₹15 = ₹3,000 + ₹500 bata = ₹3,500 + tolls.

For round trips, a minimum of 250 KM per day is billed. This is industry standard to ensure the driver earns fairly during waiting time at your destination. If the actual KM driven is less than 250 KM for the day, you'll be billed for 250 KM. For longer trips, actual distance applies.
