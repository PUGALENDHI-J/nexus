FAQs

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

How early should I book my cab?
We recommend booking at least 2–3 hours before your trip for local travel, and 24 hours ahead for outstation or airport trips. However, last-minute bookings are welcome based on availability.
What is your cancellation policy?
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before your pickup time. Cancellations within 24 hours may attract a nominal fee of ₹100–₹200 depending on the trip type.
Do you serve routes outside Madurai?
Yes! We serve all of Tamil Nadu, Pondicherry and Kerala — including Chennai, Coimbatore, Kochi, Pondicherry, Trichy, and all major cities.
Are drivers background-verified?
Every driver undergoes a thorough background check, license verification, and route training before being onboarded. Your safety is our #1 priority.
Can I track my ride live?
Yes. Once your driver is assigned, you'll receive a live tracking link via SMS and WhatsApp to share with your family for safety.
Do you offer monthly corporate packages?
Absolutely! We have dedicated corporate contracts for businesses needing daily or weekly employee transport. Contact us for a custom enquiry.
