About Us

From Hands, For Hearts

Hastavam Textiles was born from a simple belief: that clothing should carry a story, not just a size tag.

It began with a visit to a quiet weaving village, where the rhythmic clatter of handlooms echoed stories older than most cities. We watched artisans — many of them women — pour weeks into a single saree, only to see their craft fade beneath the noise of fast fashion and machine imitation. We chose to do something about it.

Today, we walk alongside artisan clusters in Varanasi, Chanderi, Kanchipuram, Jaipur and Bengal — bringing their heritage weaves straight to your wardrobe, without middlemen, without markup, and without ever losing the soul of the craft. Alongside these traditional weaves, we've built a western wear range with the same care and attention to detail.

"Manehly Handled" — With Care, By Hand, For Her

Every garment is manually inspected thread by thread, hand-finished using traditional technique, and packed as if it were being sent to family.

Our Mission

To preserve India's textile heritage by empowering artisan communities, while making traditional Indian clothing accessible, elegant and effortlessly wearable for the contemporary woman.

Our Vision

A world where every wardrobe holds at least one handwoven story — where tradition is never left behind, but worn forward, with pride.

Craftsmanship & Process

From Thread to Drape

Every Hastavam piece passes through six deliberate stages — because "manehly handled" is a promise, not a tagline.

01

Sourcing

We travel directly to weaving clusters, sourcing raw silk, cotton and dyes from trusted regional hands.

02

Weaving

Skilled artisans, many carrying forward family tradition, spend days to weeks handweaving each piece.

03

Dyeing & Printing

Traditional techniques like Bandhani, Leheriya and hand block printing bring each fabric to life.

04

Finishing

Every piece is hand-checked for thread quality, symmetry and finish before it reaches packaging.

05

Quality Assurance

Our in-house team inspects every garment, because "manehly handled" is a promise, not a tagline.

06

Packed With Care
