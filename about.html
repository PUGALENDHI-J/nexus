Our Story

Born on the Roads of South India

Saran Tours & Travels started in 2021 with a single car and a bold idea — that a taxi ride should feel like a premium experience, not a gamble. Founded in Salem, we began connecting families, professionals, and tourists to their destinations across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Pondicherry, Andhra & Telangana with reliability and care.

What began as a one-car operation quickly grew into a fleet of 50+ well-maintained vehicles as word spread about our commitment to transparency and punctuality. We don't just transport people — we create journeys worth remembering.

Today, we serve 200+ routes across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala, Pondicherry, Andhra & Telangana's major cities, airports, and tourist destinations. Every driver we hire is background-verified, route-trained, and committed to the Saran Tours & Travels standard of service.

Our tagline — "Travel · Explore · Discover" — isn't just branding. It's a promise. Every km we drive with you is a chapter in a story of safety, trust, and adventure.
